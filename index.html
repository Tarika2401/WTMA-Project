Key Insights

💡

Contract Matters

Month-to-month contracts show significantly higher churn rates. Consider offering incentives for annual commitments.

🎯

Critical First Year

Customers in their first 12 months are at highest risk. Focus retention efforts on new customer onboarding.

🛠️

Support Impact

Tech support availability correlates with lower churn. Consider expanding support services.
